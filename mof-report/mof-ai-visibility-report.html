AI Search Visibility Report

How AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI, Claude, Copilot and Perplexity describe the Ministry of Finance, and how it compares with other Saudi government entities. Data from Scrunch.

Executive summary
  • The Ministry appears in 70.2% of 3,728 AI answers, up 3.4 pts on the prior 30 days. It is usually the first entity named (average rank 1.07).
  • AI talks about the Ministry very positively: sentiment is 97.8/100, with only 10 negative answers out of 1,880 that mention it.
  • Citations are slipping. The share of answers that cite mof.gov.sa fell from 39.8% to 37.0%. Share of voice also eased, from 55.4% to 52.7%.
  • The weakest topics are Taxation, Zakat & Customs (27.5% mention, 2.7% citation), where ZATCA dominates, and Government Procurement & Tenders (44.9%).
  • mof.gov.sa is the most influential cited domain in this space. Etimad and the National Debt Management Center (NDMC) sites also rank in the top 4.

Recommendations

Suggested next steps based on the findings above.

Win back citations, not just mentions

Mentions are up but citations fell from 39.8% to 37.0%, and on Google AI Mode they fell sharply for digital services. Give each service its own clearly structured page with an FAQ, eligibility, steps and fees, so AI assistants have a definitive mof.gov.sa page to link to.

Clarify the Ministry's role in tax, zakat and customs

This is the weakest topic: 27.5% mention and 2.7% citation, with ZATCA's citations rising. Publish content that explains how the Ministry and ZATCA divide responsibility, linked from both sites, so AI describes the policy owner correctly.

Make Etimad and procurement content easier for AI to read

Procurement is at 44.9% mention, though rising on ChatGPT. Make sure the Etimad guides for suppliers, tender rules and registration steps are crawlable, current and linked to the Ministry by name.

Hold the lead on fiscal policy

PIF and SAMA are gaining on Fiscal Policy & Economic Stability. Publish budget statements, quarterly performance reports and debt and sukuk updates as HTML pages with summaries, not only PDFs, so AI cites the Ministry as the main source.

Track Arabic-language prompts

All prompts tracked in this period were in English. Adding Arabic versions would show how the Ministry appears to Saudi citizens and businesses asking in Arabic.
